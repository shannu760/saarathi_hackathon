Questions

Is the match score a chance of getting selected?

No. It measures how well your profile fits the listed rules. It is not legal eligibility or a probability of selection.

What does the AI do?

It explains results in plain language, answers questions from the records, and drafts a plan. It never decides eligibility, and any explanation that quotes numbers not in the engine output is discarded.

What if the AI is unavailable?

Every AI feature has a standard fallback built from the rules engine, clearly labelled.

Are these real listings?

Yes, the scholarships come from Buddy4Study and the internships and competitions from Unstop, retrieved on 25 Oct 2026. Rules and deadlines can change and some rules could not be retrieved, so always confirm on the official page.
